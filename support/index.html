Flaskade: common questions

I bought something but don't see it. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google Play account you used to buy.

How do I remove ads? Tap the "no ads" button on the home screen or open the Shop. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. Flaskade Plus members also see no ads.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones? Progress is backed up automatically when the game is online. Purchases can always be restored from Settings.

How do I cancel Flaskade Plus? Subscriptions are managed by Google Play: open the Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data? Email us from any address with the subject "Delete my Flaskade data". See the privacy policy for details.

Glidelock: common questions

I bought something but don't see it. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google Play account you used to buy.

How do I remove ads? Tap the "no ads" button on the home screen or in Settings. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase.

I'm stuck on a level. Tap Hint to see the next move, or let the game solve the next 5 moves. Every level can be solved in the number of moves shown at the top.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones? Progress is backed up automatically when the game is online. Purchases can always be restored from Settings.

How do I delete my data? Email us from any address with the subject "Delete my Glidelock data". See the privacy policy for details.

When you write, it helps to include your phone model and what happened.
