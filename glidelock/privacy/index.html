Glidelock privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Glidelock ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. Glidelock is made by Lantern Studio Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email us at lanternstudio.ltd@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number to play.
  • We use Google services (Firebase and AdMob) to save your progress, find and fix crashes, understand how the game is played, and show ads.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Information the app uses

WhatWhyWho handles it

Game settings such as ad frequency are downloaded with Firebase Remote Config. This does not collect extra personal data.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone itself; no information is sent to us to deliver them.

Ads and your choices

Glidelock shows ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before using the advertising identifier, and you can change this in your iPhone settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and between-level ads in the game (reward videos you choose to watch stay available).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services

These services process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy; Apple App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy

How long we keep information

Your cloud backup is kept while you play and is deleted on request. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Information on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit how it's used, and to withdraw consent. To delete your Glidelock data, email lanternstudio.ltd@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glidelock data". Because the app doesn't know who you are, we may ask for details from the game (for example your level and coin count) to find your backup. You can also stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the app.

Children

Glidelock is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Google's security protections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

International transfers

Google's services may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, with the safeguards described in Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
