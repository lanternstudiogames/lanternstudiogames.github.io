MindLantern privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information the brain-training app MindLantern ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. MindLantern is made by Lantern Studio Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email us at lanternstudio.ltd@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Your progress then stays on your phone only.
  • If you create an account (email, Google or Apple), we keep your progress and profile in it so you can use them on any phone. You can delete your account at any time from the app.
  • MindLantern has no ads.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

MindLantern is for fun and everyday mental fitness. Its scores are not a medical or psychological assessment.

Your account

An account is optional. When you sign out, the app saves your latest progress to your account and removes your progress and profile from the phone; signing in again brings them back. To delete your account and everything stored in it, open your profile in the app and tap Delete account. This also clears your progress from the phone.

Third-party services

These services process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sign in with Apple and the App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy

How long we keep information

Your account and the progress in it are kept until you delete the account in the app or ask us to delete it. Information on your phone is removed when you sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit how it's used, and to withdraw consent. You can change your profile in the app at any time and delete your account from your profile. You can also email lanternstudio.ltd@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MindLantern data" from the email address of your account, and we'll delete it for you.

Children

MindLantern is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Google's security protections. Each account can only read and change its own data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

International transfers

Your account data is stored by Google in the European Union. Google's services may also process data in other countries, including the United States, with the safeguards described in Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes, for example if we add crash reports or usage statistics to improve the app. We'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
